Manxing Gravity — Music · Film · Game

Manxing Gravity is a comprehensive cultural enterprise driven by music creation, game development and film production — focused on IP incubation, content creation and full-chain operations. Based in Wuhan, China, we build worlds across every medium.

Original scores become the soul of cinematic worlds, game engines render stories you can step into, and films bring characters to life. Every creation is connected — music binds them into a single, unified universe.

Core Verticals

  • Music Production — full-chain capability from composition and arrangement to audio post-production.
  • Game Development — full-stack development powered by Unreal Engine, from ReverseRealm (UE4) to immersive VR concert experiences (UE5).
  • Film Production — animation, web films and short-form drama with a full professional pipeline, represented by our 12-episode original series.
  • Virtual IP — original virtual characters with registered copyrights, powered by an AI Agent-driven organizational structure.

Company Overview

  • 3 creative verticals
  • 10+ original works
  • 100% IP synergy — game IP to film, film IP to game

关于漫星引力
